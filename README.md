# Twisted Toroids
An interactive knot and surface lab by Mac Mayo.

## Projects

- [Twisted Toroids](twisted-toroids/) — twist, cut, and trace painted surfaces, inspect linked flat maps, and solve construction challenges. Draw closed braids by word or in a diagram editor, name them from a table of knots up to 10 crossings by their Jones polynomial (handedness included), see their Seifert surfaces, and relax them into smooth knots. Cut a torus or a Klein bottle along loops you draw, and download any model as an STL for 3D printing. Six paint palettes and light/dark backgrounds let you customize the view.

Open the GitHub Pages site at `/knots/twisted-toroids/`, or serve the `twisted-toroids/` folder locally (see its README). Previous app links redirect to the new address, including shared construction parameters.
